# Make AI work for you: fine-tuning launches on Nebius AI Studio

Source: https://nebius.com/blog/posts/fine-tuning-launches-on-nebius-ai-studio

Skip to main content

Nebius AI Studio is now Nebius Token Factory: same platform, new name, more power for running AI at scale.

Generic AI models are powerful, but they often fall short when it comes to specialized tasks. Specialized tasks require complex prompts, consume large context windows, and still might miss the mark on domain-specific requirements. Fine-tuning solves these challenges by teaching models to understand your unique requirements, terminology and workflows.

### The power of customizationThe power of customization

With fine-tuning on Nebius AI Studio, you can transform generic models into specialized solutions that truly understand your domain. Instead of relying on massive models with generic knowledge, you can create smaller, more efficient models that excel at your specific tasks. This means better accuracy, lower costs and more consistent outputs across all interactions.

Our platform brings together an extensive selection of more than 30 leading models, including the latest Llama 3 series (1B to 70B), Qwen series (1.5B to 72B), and specialized models like DeepSeek Coder. All models support LoRA fine-tuning, with full fine-tuning available for models with less than 20B parameters, giving you complete control over your customization approach.

### Built for productionBuilt for production

We’ve developed our fine-tuning service with production workloads in mind. Our high-performance GPU clusters scale automatically with your needs, while FlashAttention-3 support ensures optimal training speed. The platform is fully compatible with the OpenAI SDK, making integration seamless for teams already working with AI.

When it comes to deployment, flexibility is key. Choose between serverless deployment, via Nebius AI Studio, for maximum convenience, on-demand Nebius Cloud GPU instances for specific workloads or reserved clusters for production scale. This flexibility ensures your fine-tuned models can grow with your needs.

### Simple, transparent pricingSimple, transparent pricing

We believe in making advanced AI capabilities accessible. Our simplified usage-based pricing means you pay only for the tokens processed during training, with no hidden costs or additional charges for model deployment. Pricing scales with your model and training data size, ensuring you get the best value for your investment.

### Getting startedGetting started

Fine-tuning your first model is straightforward. Select your base model from our comprehensive catalog, choose between LoRA and full fine-tuning, configure your training parameters and deploy your specialized model. Our platform handles the complexity, letting you focus on building better AI solutions.
