## Why teams move

Self-managed inference scales into platform work. A custom serving stack gives control at the beginning. At scale, it can become a second roadmap that competes with the product roadmap. Teams usually feel it first through:

- 

Capacity planning that keeps changing

- 

Latency targets that are hard to hold

- 

Model upgrades that create risk

- 

Missing cost and usage visibility

- 

Support handoffs across infra, product, and customer teams

- 

On-call load from serving problems that are not core product differentiation

## Questions and answers

Is this only for teams that want to stop self-managing entirely?

No. The first step is deciding what your team should keep, what should move, and what should be optimized.

Do we need to migrate everything at once?

No. Start with one workload where cost, latency, reliability, or roadmap drag is already visible.

What should we bring to the review?

Bring the current model path, serving stack, token or request volume, latency targets, and what is currently breaking first.

Can this support dedicated production needs?

Dedicated endpoint paths can be part of the recommendation when the workload requires predictable capacity, isolation, or closer operational planning.
