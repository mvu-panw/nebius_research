## Why join

Fellows get exclusive access, perks, and opportunities!

### Access

Early or exclusive access to products, credits, and technical resources.

### Visibility

Grow your presence through speaking, content, and community.

### Network

Connect with top AI builders, founders, and engineers.

### Opportunities

Collaborate with the Nebius DevRel team on events and initiatives.

### Support

Get direct access to the team building the platform.

### Community

Join a curated network of ambitious AI builders worldwide.
