Eliminate glue code between simulation, training, and deployment. With support for SDK and CLI, Physical AI Workbench by Nebius defines your full physical AI pipeline in one YAML manifest with automated dependency management, retries, and artifact passing across every stage.

## Synthetic data generation

Turn limited real-world footage into massive, diverse training datasets. NVIDIA Cosmos on Nebius generates physics-grounded synthetic variations, including weather, lighting, rare edge cases that augment data to close the long-tail gaps field collection can never cover.

## VLA and world model training

Train vision-language-action and world foundation models on best-in-class Nebius Grace Blackwell and Blackwell clusters. With 104%+ performance on NVIDIA benchmarks1 and 12 minutes for MTTR2, jobs finish faster — helping you save on costs.

## Large-scale robot simulation

Run simulation rollouts with NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 instances purpose-built for physical AI. Validate robot behaviors across thousands of parallel virtual environments and reduce pricing by up to 75% by using preemptible instances3.

## Vision AI and video analytics

Fine-tune VLMs on curated real-world video for industrial monitoring, smart cities, and safety applications. Nebius provides sustained GPU access, annotation pipelines, and managed inference, cutting the time from raw footage to production-ready model.

## Autonomous vehicle development

Use one platform for ingesting petabyte-scale sensor data, generating synthetic driving scenarios with NVIDIA Cosmos, and training perception and planning models. Run safety validation in simulation before committing to real-world testing.

## What we offer for physical AI

## NVIDIA OSMO Managed by Nebius

Connect simulation, training, and edge deployment in one YAML-defined workflow. No Kubernetes expertise, no glue code needed. Just use a single managed control plane for your entire physical AI pipeline.

## Physical AI Data Factory

Run the NVIDIA Physical AI Data Factory Blueprint on Nebius. Curate real-world data, augment with NVIDIA Cosmos, and automatically validate outputs before they touch your training set.

## Physical AI Workbench

Easily build, train, and deploy physical AI systems. An open source initiative from Nebius, Workbench packages containerized tools, SkyPilot workflows, CLI commands, and SDK entry points.

## Serverless GPU compute

Use serverless to provision GPUs in under 3 minutes for simulations, synthetic data generation, and policy training. Pay only for what you use. No reserved clusters, no idle costs between runs.

## Choice of orchestration

Flexible orchestration for every physical AI workload — NVIDIA OSMO, Managed Slurm, Managed Kubernetes, and pre-integrated Anyscale, SkyPilot, and Run:ai — all on one platform with unified GPU scheduling.
