## Why custom AI starts with the loop

The model call is not the product moat. Every team can prompt the same frontier models. The advantage comes from the system around the model: production traces, failed tasks, evals, domain knowledge, training workflows, and deployment back into the product.

The loop looks like this:

Capture production traces

Turn failures into evals and datasets

Train or tune the right model artifact

Deploy through the right endpoint path

Measure quality, latency, and economics

Repeat as the product changes

## Questions and answers

Do we need production traces already?

They help, but the first step can be mapping what signal exists today and what should be collected next.

Is this just fine-tuning?

No. Fine-tuning may be one answer. The right answer could also be better evals, reinforcement fine-tuning, Custom Speculator, bring-your-own-weights, endpoint changes, or no training yet.

When does Custom Speculator fit?

It fits when the workload has recurring prompt shapes and serving performance can improve through a drafter trained for that traffic.

Can this lower cost?

It can identify cost-improvement paths, but the public promise should stay conservative: improve the workload across quality, latency, and economics without unsupported savings claims.
