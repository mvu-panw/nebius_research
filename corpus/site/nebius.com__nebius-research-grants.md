## How to apply

## Submit your proposal

Applications are open year-round.

Complete proposals submitted by the last day of each month will enter that month’s review cycle.

## Application review

If you submit your proposal in a given month, we will review it during the next calendar month.

We may contact you if we need additional information.

## Decision

Successful applicants will be notified by the end of the next calendar month.

If we haven’t contacted you by the end of the next calendar month, your proposal was not selected.

You’re welcome to revise it and reapply in a future cycle.

## FAQ

If my request wasn’t approved, can I apply again next time?

Yes, you can apply again with another research project.

Can multiple individuals from the same research proposal apply?

If you are working on the same research, only one person from your team can apply. Credits will be granted to this individual.

Can I apply for more credits for the same project?

No, you are only eligible to apply one time for the Research Credits Program.

Submitting an application does not guarantee selection or an award. Selection and the amount of any award are determined at Nebius’ discretion, subject to resource availability. Selected applicants will receive the applicable program terms for review and acceptance. Credits will be provided only after acceptance of those terms and successful completion of onboarding and required identity and eligibility checks.

The offer is valid for a limited time only.

The offer cannot be combined with other promotions, discounts or special offers.

Any conditions of this offer are subject to change.

Cloud credits are provided at Nebius’ sole discretion.
