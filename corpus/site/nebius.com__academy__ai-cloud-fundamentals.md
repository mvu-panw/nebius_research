## Explore AI Cloud

The course gives you an understanding of how AI cloud infrastructure works as a system: how compute, storage, networking, and GPU fabrics fit together, why they work the way they do, and how infrastructure choices affect real workloads.

This course is designed for learners familiar with core cloud computing concepts and services. Hands-on experience with general-purpose cloud platforms is an advantage, but is not essential.
