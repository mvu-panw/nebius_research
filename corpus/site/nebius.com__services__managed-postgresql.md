## Managed Service for PostgreSQL: FAQ

What is PostgreSQL?

PostgreSQL is an open-source, object-relational Database Management System (DBMS) that is highly reliable, easy to scale and often used as a backend database for ML tools.

What version of PostgreSQL do you support?

Managed Service for PostgreSQL supports PostgreSQL 16.

Should I configure a VM to make this product running?

No, all infrastructure components including Compute (virtual machines) are provisioned automatically.

How often can I make backups for the service?

You can setup one checkpoint per day within the established retention period. For access to a specific backup version from a particular time, contact our support team.

Do you provide SLA for the product?

Yes. Check the documentation to see the SLAs.
