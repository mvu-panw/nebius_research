## Report an abuse

If you believe our services are being utilized for any abusive activities, please notify us by completing this form.

To help us investigate and resolve the issue as quickly as possible, please fill all the required fields.

## Point of contact for Member States’ authorities, the Commission, and the Board

As per Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act) Member States’ authorities, the Commission and the Board may contact Nebius at: dsa@nebius.com

This point of contact shall only be used by the mentioned authorities and communications, including orders under articles 9 and 10 of the Digital Services Act, must be either be in English or Dutch, or include an English or Dutch translation.
