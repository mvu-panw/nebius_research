## Learn the skills you need to start working

The course is designed to help new users get comfortable quickly, complete meaningful tasks early, and build toward more advanced AI and infrastructure workflows step by step.

## Navigate the Nebius AI console

Understand projects, services, and where to start building in minutes.

## Launch your first cloud resources

Create a storage bucket, launch a virtual machine, configure access, and set up a development environment for practical work.

## Deploy and Run your LLM

Deploy an LLM, run inference, and test results right in the console.

## Get results in 30 min

Complete real tasks and see outcomes. No long theory upfront.

## A strong starting point for anyone getting hands-on with Nebius Cloud

A strong starting point for anyone getting hands-on with Nebius AI Cloud. It is especially helpful for developers, ML engineers, data specialists, technical students, and teams who want a clear path from first access to productive use of the platform.

## No heavy prerequisites

You do not need deep platform knowledge to start. The course introduces concepts in a practical order.

## Hands-on practice

Each part is built around actions you can take yourself, not just descriptions of services and features.

## Customer-focused outcomes

By the end, you should be able to launch resources, manage storage, configure access, and understand your next step.

## Self-paced and reusable

Learn at your own speed and return to the materials when you need a refresher while building in the platform.
