# Innovating patient journeys with AI solutions to reduce potentially avoidable hospitalizations

Healthcare innovators and technology leaders discussed, in a roundtable organized by Nebius, AstraZeneca, and NVIDIA, opportunities for health systems to augment care delivery and reduce Potentially Avoidable Hospitalizations (PAHs) with AI.

This whitepaper summarizes expert insights on how growing medical data and AI capabilities help clinicians and nurses scale guideline-directed medical therapy (GDMT) across patient journeys to reduce PAHs.

Explore views on:

- 

AI’s potential to help care providers personalize treatments and extend specialist capabilities to the frontlines with case-specific analytics and clinically validated agentic and generative solutions.

- 

Opportunities to advance implementation by addressing barriers around data availability, clinical adoption, system-wide incentives, and regulatory clarity.

- 

Pathways for health systems, pharmaceutical leaders, tech companies, and policymakers to collaborate and enable AI-enhanced disease management.
