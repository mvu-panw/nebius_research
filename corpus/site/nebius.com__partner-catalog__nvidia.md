Production AI, co-engineered with NVIDIA

Nebius and NVIDIA collaborate across the full stack—from AI factory design and accelerated computing to cloud software, models, and production applications. Together, they help customers train, deploy and operate AI at scale.

Joint engineering delivers validated performance: Nebius holds NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud recognition for training on HGX H200, HGX B300, and GB300 NVL72, alongside Platinum in SemiAnalysis ClusterMax 3.0.

The collaboration also supports early collaboration on new products, including NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 and NVIDIA Spectrum-X networking, with plans to adopt NVIDIA Groq 3 LPX with Vera Rubin in Token Factory for faster agent inference.

Full stack collaboration: open models and workflows

Together, we bring AI applications into production. Nebius integrates NVIDIA open models, software, and blueprints with Nebius AI Cloud services and Token Factory, helping customers turn reference architectures into AI workflows they can deploy and operate.

- Agents: Nebius Agents Blueprint combines NVIDIA Nemotron with LangChain for retrieval and observability.

- Retail: Shopping assistants and catalog enrichment, plus inventory, store operations, and warehouse blueprints.

- Robotics: Physical AI Workbench integrates NVIDIA Cosmos Data Factory and Isaac tools for data generation, simulation, and training.

- Life sciences: NVIDIA’s virtual-screening blueprint on Nebius connects protein prediction, molecule generation, and docking.

Learn and build with Nebius Academy and NVIDIA DLI

Nebius Academy, the education and research arm of Nebius, and NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute collaborate on AI education, co-developing courses and making select NVIDIA DLI courseware available through the Nebius Academy platform.

Experts from both companies built the first co-developed course, Agentic AI, covering NVIDIA Nemotron models, Nebius Token Factory, and Tavily. Learning and building in the same Nebius environment gives developers a direct path from experimentation to production.
