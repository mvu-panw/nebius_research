## FAQ

How does the Nebius for Startups program work?

Nebius for Startups is a highly selective program offering AI founders explusive partnership including exclusive compute credits, engineering support, and go-to-market opportunities. Acceptance is by application through a Nebius VC partner only.

Our founder community is free and open to all AI startups globally. Use the form above to join and learn more about future events and our expanding founder programming.

Who can apply?

The Nebius for Startups program is highly selective and accessible only through a Nebius VC partner and requires explicit acceptance by our team. To be considered, contact your fund’s platform team to confirm they are a qualified partner. Startups eligible for program consideration must also meet the following criteria:

• VC or accelerator-backed

• Have raised at least $5M+ USD from one of our approved VC partners

• Building or scaling an AI-enabled product or model

• Maintain a working company website

• Apply using a company email address

• Officially incorporated fewer than 5 years ago

    ‎

Companies that are not eligible include:

• Consulting or outsourced development firms

• Resellers or distributors

• Cloud service providers

• Cryptocurrency-only businesses

• Public companies

How long does approval take?

Due to overwhelming demand, we currently cannot guarantee response times. For this reason, we recommend applying only when ready. Applicants will be directed to our waitlist for future consideration. The Nebius for Startups team will reach out to inform you if your organization has been moved off the waitlist. Access to other available Startup Program offerings remains open to qualifying startup applicants, regardless of waitlist status.

To stay in touch and receive useful updates and program news please join our founder community.

Can I use Nebius for just part of my stack?

Yes. We’re here to support you whether you want to start small, or go all-in.

Try Nebius AI Cloud for training and large-scale workloads, or Nebius Token Factory for fast, cost-efficient inference using open source.
