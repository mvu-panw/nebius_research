# Game on! UFB and Nebius power the future of robotics

Source: https://nebius.com/blog/posts/ufb-and-nebius-power-the-future-of-robotics

Skip to main content

We are thrilled to announce that Nebius is the official technology partner for Season 1 of Ultimate Fighting Bots (UFB)’s Humanoid Sports League — the world’s first multi-format competition platform for physical AI. The two companies will pioneer the use of robots by coupling UFB’s growing platform for humanoids with Nebius’s cloud solution for physical AI.

As part of the upcoming competitions, teams will train, pilot and choreograph humanoid robots across multiple disciplines, including robot fights and dance.

## Robots on stage. Nebius under the hoodRobots on stage. Nebius under the hood

Robotics demands more than off-the-shelf compute. Robot learning needs fast iterations, reliable cloud services and the kind of acceleration that can keep pace with real-time decision-making. Nebius delivers the critical cloud foundation for robot policies, simulation workloads, benchmark scoring and live-event inference that humanoids need to operate on stage.

Along with the launch of the Humanoid Sports League, Nebius cloud for robotics and physical AI is powering the first creative tool for human motion, UFB Studio. Available at studio.ultimatebots.com, UFB Studio allows anyone to upload reference video, generate motion and refine output in a timeline editor built specifically for humanoid bodies. Submissions, including performances, immersive experiences and showcases will be featured live on stage by robots at the league’s events. Submissions will also remain available for community use.

## Mark your calendar. Season 1 is comingMark your calendar. Season 1 is coming

As the cloud for robotics and physical AI, Nebius will be behind every movement, every reaction, every moment of machine intelligence during UFB’s action-packed season. Founders, developers, investors and all physical AI enthusiasts are in for something extraordinary. Expect robots performing on stage in ways that defy expectations.

### May 14 — Fight x Dance at Temple SFMay 14 — Fight x Dance at Temple SF

Season 1 kicks off at Temple SF (San Francisco) with “Fight x Dance, ” a debut event that combines robot fight matches and dance competitions in a single electrifying show. Robot pilots include winners of the recent ghost trials at UC Berkeley and other notable guests. Doors open at 7:00 PM PT, with the show running from 7:30 to 10:00 PM.

### June 25–26 — San Francisco Bay AreaJune 25–26 — San Francisco Bay Area

The action continues in the Bay Area with a two-day event bringing together robot pilots, engineers, creators and audiences around the emerging culture of humanoid robotics.

### July 18 — San Francisco Bay AreaJuly 18 — San Francisco Bay Area

A third event will continue to raise the stakes as teams push their robots further across live competition formats, performances and immersive showcases.

### August / September — Season 1 FinaleAugust / September — Season 1 Finale

Season 1 will culminate in a grand finale event that will bring the entire UFB community together for a landmark moment in physical AI, all powered by Nebius.
