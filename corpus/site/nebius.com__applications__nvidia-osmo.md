# NVIDIA OSMO
by NVIDIA
Orchestration

NVIDIA Osmo is a cloud-native orchesation and workflow management platform designed to streamline robotics and AI development across simulation and real-world environments. It enables teams to coordinate simulation jobs, synthetic data generation, model training, validation, and deployment pipelines through a unified interface. By integrating with NVIDIA’s simulation and AI ecosystem, Osmo helps automate complex, distributed workloads while ensuring scalability, reproducibility, and efficient resource utilization in the cloud.Deploy on Nebius
