# Viewing usage data in Nebius AI Cloud

You can monitor how much you spend with [pay-as-you-go (PAYG)](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/billing-models/payg.md) and [commitment discounts](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/billing-models/committed-usage.md) on the corresponding pages under the [Billing](https://console.nebius.com/billing) section in the web console. View the usage dashboards and filter data to catch unexpected spikes or underutilization. To get an email alert when your spending crosses an alert threshold you set, create a [budget](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/budgets.md).

## How to view PAYG usage

You can track your spending on different projects, services and resources under **Billing** → **Usage** in the web console.

If you have both PAYG and commitment discounts, the **Usage** column shows both types of usage combined. The **Total**, **Subtotal**, **VAT** and **Cost** values are calculated only from PAYG usage.

Use the following controls to explore the data:

* To filter the usage, apply the region, project, service and product filters.
* To view detailed charts by services or resources, click  **Chart** and select a grouping and period. To see the usage details for a specific period, hover over its column in the chart.

You can also track how costs are distributed across different teams, applications or projects. To do so, assign labels that represent the breakdown to resources. For more information, see [Cost allocation labels in Nebius AI Cloud](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/usage/cost-allocation-labels.md).

## How to view commitment discounts

To track your spending on different services and resources:

1. In the [web console](https://console.nebius.com), go to  [Billing](https://console.nebius.com/billing) → **Discounts**.
2. View the usage details on the **Commitment discounts** tab.

Unpaid orders appear in the commitment discounts list with the  sign.

Use the following controls to explore the data:

* To filter the usage, use the region, service and product filters.

* To view a detailed chart by services and resources, click  **Chart**. The data in the chart is calculated hourly. To see the usage details for a specific period, hover over a usage column.

  The chart includes usage covered by commitment discounts, and overconsumption that is billed as PAYG. You can also see if you had underutilized resource usage, marked as **Underconsumption**.
