# Nebius achieves NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud on NVIDIA HGX B300 for training

Source: https://nebius.com/blog/posts/nebius-achieves-nvidia-exemplar-cloud-on-nvidia-hgx-b300-for-training

Skip to main content

Nebius has achieved NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud on NVIDIA HGX B300 for training workloads. NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud recognizes cloud platforms that meet NVIDIA’s performance requirements across a defined suite of AI workloads. NVIDIA validated our production infrastructure using its Performance Benchmarking recipes, evaluating training performance against its reference architecture.

This achievement follows our Exemplar Cloud validations on NVIDIA H200 and NVIDIA HGX GB300 NVL72.

## How NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud validates cloud performanceHow NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud validates cloud performance

Cloud providers run NVIDIA’s standardized benchmarking recipes, measure results against its reference performance, and submit those results to NVIDIA for review. Nebius and NVIDIA engineers tested our production infrastructure, reviewed the results, and refined the platform configuration.

Each GPU platform requires its own validation. Achieving Exemplar on HGX B300 extends our work with the NVIDIABlackwell Ultra platform available on Nebius AI Cloud. This result builds on the engineering approach behind our Exemplar validation on NVIDIA H200 and NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 achievements: designing, operating, and optimizing hardware and cloud software together for customer workloads.

## What repeat external validation means for training on NebiusWhat repeat external validation means for training on Nebius

Exemplar is a strong signal that Nebius’ HGX B300 infrastructure is ready for the most demanding production training workloads, backed by NVIDIA’s validation of training performance. For customers planning demanding training runs, the external validation provides greater confidence in the platform, supported by measured workload performance.

Beyond Exemplar, separate evaluations provide additional evidence of our infrastructure’s performance and reliability. In MLPerf® Training 6.0, Nebius posted the fastest single-node NVIDIA HGX B300 times for Llama-3.1-8B and GPT-OSS 20B in the closed division. SemiAnalysis recently rated Nebius Platinum in ClusterMAX™ 3.0, its highest tier, following testing of training and inference workloads, storage, and failure recovery on live NVIDIA HGX B300 and GB300 NVL72 clusters.
