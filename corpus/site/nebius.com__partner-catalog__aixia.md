# AIXIA

AIXIA helps organizations design, deploy, and operate infrastructure for AI development and production. Its expertise spans GPU computing, high-performance storage, networking, and data centre operations, with support from initial architecture and technology selection through deployment and ongoing management.

Combining infrastructure engineering with software development and data science, AIXIA helps teams move from proof of concept to practical AI applications. Its work includes computer vision, natural language processing, and predictive maintenance, with solutions tailored to each organization’s workloads, requirements, and budget.
