# Trademark Usage Guidelines

## What’s allowed

Nebius brand assets are protected by law and you must seek permission to use them from Nebius Group. Do not use Nebius brand assets without written consent from Nebius Group, except under the following circumstances:

- 

Licensed Use: Authorized licensees may use Nebius brand assets pursuant to a valid, executed license agreement with Nebius Group. All such use must comply with both the terms set forth in that agreement and these guidelines.

- 

Authorized resellers and/or distributors: Approved resellers and distributors may use Nebius brand assets solely to advertise, promote, and sell genuine Nebius Group products or services, subject to the terms of their distribution or reseller agreement.

- 

Editorial and Media Use: Journalists, publishers, and media outlets may use Nebius brand assets in articles, news reports and commentary.

- 

Educational and Academic Use: Nebius brand assets may be used in academic, non-commercial contexts, including scholarly research, lectures, or textbooks.

- 

Referential Use (Nominative Fair Use): Third parties may refer to Nebius brand assets to identify Nebius Group products or services (e.g. in compatibility statements), provided that:

- 

The use is necessary to identify the product or service;

- 

No suggestion of endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation is made;

- 

Nebius brand assets are not used more prominently than necessary.

- 

Sponsorship, Certification, and Co-Branded Activities: Use of Nebius brand assets in connection with sponsorships, certifications, endorsements, or co-branded marketing campaigns is permitted only with prior written authorization from Nebius Group, and subject to the applicable terms both specified in the written authorization and as set forth in these guidelines.

- 

Event Participation: Authorized participants in Nebius Group events or programs (e.g., vendors, speakers, certified instructors) may use Nebius brand assets to indicate their involvement, provided such use is pre-approved in writing and consistent with these guidelines.

All uses of Nebius brand assets whether authorized or referencing, must follow the below general guidelines.

## 

Dos

1. Use Nebius brand assets Accurately: Always use Nebius brand assets exactly as provided without modifying their design, color, or proportions. Ensure that Nebius brand assets are always represented consistently and clearly.

2. Maintain Context: Use Nebius brand assets in a way that truthfully refers to Nebius Group or its products/services, avoiding misleading implications of endorsement.

3. Proper linking and mentions: When mentioning or linking to Nebius brand assets or Nebius Group, make sure to do so accurately and in an appropriate context. Ensure links direct to the official websites and resources of Nebius Group (e.g. www.nebius.com) and not cached or mirrored sites.

4. Request permission when necessary: If you’re unsure about using Nebius brand assets or their elements, seek explicit authorization at trademarkusage@nebius.com. If the usage deviates from the basic intended purpose (whether authorized or referencing), please contact Nebius Group to obtain express permission.

## 

Don’ts

1. Do Not Register Nebius brand assets in Domain Names or Business Names: Avoid using Nebius brand assets as part of your domain name, website, or company name.

2. Do Not Modify Nebius brand assetss: Never alter Nebius brand assets by resizing disproportionately, changing colors, abbreviating, creating derivative versions, misspelling or incorporating them into taglines or slogans.

3. Do Not Use Nebius brand assets Descriptively: Avoid using Nebius brand assets as generic terms or in a descriptive sense.

4. Do Not Imply Endorsement: Do not use Nebius brand assets in a way that suggests approval, certification, or sponsorship by Nebius Group unless explicitly authorized. Do not give the impression that Nebius Group supports your products or services without permission.

5. Do not use Nebius brand assets in an offensive or defamatory way: Avoid using Nebius brand assets in a way that could create negative associations or misunderstandings. Do not use their representations or colors in inappropriate or controversial content.

6. Do Not Use on Merchandise Without Permission: Avoid placing Nebius brand assets on items like T-shirts, mugs, bags or accessories unless approved in writing by Nebius Group.

7. Do not combine Nebius brand assets with other brands without permission: Do not mix Nebius Brand Asset with others or use them alongside other entities without obtaining formal permission. Nebius brand assets should always be represented clearly, distinctly and independently.

## Ownership

Nebius brand assets are the exclusive property of Nebius Group or its affiliates and partners. The use of Nebius brand assets does not grant any rights or title to them, and all rights not explicitly granted under these guidelines are reserved by Nebius Group. Any and all goodwill arising from the use of Nebius brand assets shall inure solely to the benefit of Nebius Group.

## Legal actions

Nebius Group reserves the right to take appropriate legal action in the event of an infringement or misappropriation of its intellectual property rights related to its Nebius brand assets. This includes filing legal claims for damages or seeking injunctive relief to prevent unauthorized use of the trademarks.

## Modifications to the Guidelines

Nebius Group reserves the right to modify these guidelines at any time. Updates or modifications will be effective once posted on the official website of the company. Users are encouraged to review these guidelines periodically to stay informed of any changes.

## Reporting misuse

Any suspected misuse or unauthorized use of Nebius brand assets should be reported immediately to our legal team at trademarkreport@nebius.com. Please provide a description of the misuse, where it was found (e.g., website URL, product packaging), and a screenshot or supporting documentation if available.
