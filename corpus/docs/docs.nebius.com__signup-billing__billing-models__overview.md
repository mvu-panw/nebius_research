# Billing models in Nebius AI Cloud

Nebius AI Cloud has two billing models:

* [Pay-as-you-go model (PAYG)](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/billing-models/payg.md) offers cost-effective flexibility and fits variable or unpredictable workloads. It is available to individuals and companies.
* [Commitment discounts model](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/billing-models/committed-usage.md) provides discounts for committing to specific Nebius AI Cloud usage and fits stable, long-term workloads. It is only available to companies.

PAYG is the default billing model: it covers all usage that is not included in your commitment discounts. You can combine the models based on your usage patterns and business needs; in this case, you will pay separately for PAYG and commitment discounts.
