# Dynatrace

Observability

Dynatrace provides unified observability for GenAI and Agentic AI workloads running on Nebius infrastructure. From the first user prompt through models, agents, orchestration layers, and underlying infrastructure resources, Dynatrace delivers deep visibility into performance, cost, quality, and reliability. AI engineering and platform teams gain insight into model behavior, agent decision paths, prompt chains, retrieval pipelines, and infrastructure utilization. This enables faster debugging, safer production deployments, continuous quality evaluation, and confident scaling of AI systems while maintaining governance and compliance requirements.

Dynatrace is available in the Nebius Marketplace as a partner-managed application, providing observability for GenAI and agentic workloads on Nebius AI Cloud — from the first user prompt through model invocations, agent decision paths, token usage, and GPU utilization, with guardrails and audit trails.

Nebius AI Cloud includes built-in observability; Dynatrace adds depth for organizations that standardize on its platform across their environments.
