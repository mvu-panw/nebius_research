# Hobson Infrastructure

Hobson Infrastructure is a data centre capacity brokerage and engineering consultancy based in Dubai and Geneva. It helps neoclouds, AI companies, hyperscalers, and investors source GPU and data centre capacity across Europe, the Middle East, India, and the Americas, with delivery support spanning commissioning, project management, and on-site engineering.

As a Nebius referral partner, Hobson Infrastructure connects clients seeking compute for AI and high-performance computing workloads with Nebius AI Cloud.
