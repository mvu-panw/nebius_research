# Explorer Tier: Investing in AI entrepreneurs

Source: https://nebius.com/blog/posts/h100-explorer-tier

Skip to main content

Nebius is committed to supporting and investing in the AI entrepreneur community. We sponsor hackathons and interact with the community of startups, both big and small, on a daily basis. With up to 70% of AI funding being spent on compute, we still recognize the challenges that high GPU costs pose for getting a project off the ground.

We also see the amazing things that hackers can accomplish in a short time, often with as few as 1–2 H100 GPUs. However, securing access to GPUs can be challenging, with limited availability of H100 in some regions and the requirement to use full hosts with 8 GPUs. Some clients report needing to keep their instances running 24/7 to avoid stockouts, making on-demand usage difficult, if not impossible, in practice.

### What we offerWhat we offer

Simply sign up and use up to 8 GPUs at a time, whenever you need them. The first 1,000 H100 GPU-hours would cost you only 1.50 USD per hour. We offer the lowest price on the market, with genuine self-service. No commitments. No stockouts.

The Explorer Tier is valid for all new customers who join after October 1, 2024. These prices are valid until the end of 2024, and the offer may be extended into 2025 or adjusted based on customer demand and feedback.

### How it worksHow it works

Let’s take a look at the table below, illustrating in detail how these conditions work.

For example, if you deploy one VM with one NVIDIA H100 GPU at 00:00 on November 1 and run it the entire month (720 hours), you’re within the first 1,000 hours of the Explorer Tier, so the price is 1.5 USD per GPU/hour. This price includes CPU and RAM. The total cost at the end of the month will be 1,080 USD.

If you deploy two VMs under the same conditions, your total GPU operating time will be 1,440 hours. The Explorer Tier price applies to the first 1,000 hours, and the remaining 440 hours will be charged at the standard on-demand price of 2.95 USD per hour. In this case, the total cost will be 2,798 USD (1,500 USD + 1,298 USD).

Please keep in mind that all storage will be billed separately.

### Why are we doing this?Why are we doing this?

At Nebius, we firmly believe that our long-term success is intertwined with the success of the AI entrepreneur community. It’s in our best interest to support those bold enough to try to change the world — because they’re the ones who do.

We want to be part of your success. By offering market-leading pricing when you’re just getting started, we aim to ease your path to a working product, funding and revenue. In the meantime, we hope you’ll experience our full-stack platform and make it your weapon of choice by the time you’re ready to scale.
