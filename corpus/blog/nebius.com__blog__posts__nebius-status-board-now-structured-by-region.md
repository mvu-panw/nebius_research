# Nebius Status Board: now structured by region

Source: https://nebius.com/blog/posts/nebius-status-board-now-structured-by-region

Skip to main content

We are introducing updates to the Nebius Status Board to enhance transparency during service outages and provide customers with a clearer view of Nebius AI Cloud service availability.

# Regional supportRegional support

With this update, the Nebius Status Board (status.nebius.com) now includes regional views, allowing you to monitor the operational status of platform services for each geographic region individually.

If there is a service degradation or scheduled maintenance, you’ll be able to see exactly which regions and services are affected, helping you quickly assess the impact and stay informed.

# Event history before Oct 27Event history before Oct 27

As part of the migration required to introduce regional views, all incidents created before October 27, 2025 have been assigned to the eu-north1 region, regardless of the actual location where the event occurred.

As a result, the uptime value for eu-north1 region reflects all service degradation and downtime periods across all Nebius AI Cloud regions prior to that date. For all other regions, uptime values are calculated based only on incidents that occurred on or after October 27, 2025.
