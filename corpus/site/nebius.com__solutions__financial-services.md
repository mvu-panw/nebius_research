## Capital markets

Train proprietary models and run compute-heavy analytics over market data on GPU clusters built for low latency and massive parallel simulation.

Capabilities:

- Serve trading analytics at low latency on dedicated endpoints

- Run risk simulation on InfiniBand GPU clusters that scale on demand

- Ground research in live market data with built-in agentic search

- Log every step with compliance agents built for audit

## Quant research

Discover, test and deploy systematic strategies faster, on GPU capacity that scales for the run and an open-model stack already validated for finance.

Capabilities:

- Optimize portfolios with GPU-accelerated Mean-CVaR on dedicated clusters

- Run agentic signal-discovery loops on open models through Token Factory

- Classify news and events at scale on cost-efficient open models

- Backtest across decades of history on clusters that spin up and release on demand

## Banking

Train models on your own customer data and serve them in production at the scale of millions of daily transactions and support requests.

Capabilities:

- Detect fraud with transaction models trained on your data, served live

- Run customer service chat on open models through Token Factory

- Reuse one credit-scoring model across scoring, risk and cross-sell

- Personalize and cross-sell from one shared foundation model

## Insurance

Turn policy, claims and risk data into faster decisions with models you train and own, supported by auditable human-in-the-loop agents.

Capabilities:

- Train underwriting models on your loss history and own the weights

- Run claims agents that read documents, verify sources and escalate edge cases

- Ground customer-service assistants in your own documents

- Detect fraud and anomalies across claims at scale
