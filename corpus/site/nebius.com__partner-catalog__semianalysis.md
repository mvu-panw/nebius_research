# SemiAnalysis

Infrastructure
Models

SemiAnalysis independently evaluates GPU clouds through its ClusterMAX rating system, in which Nebius holds a Gold rating (ClusterMAX 2.0). Nebius also supplies compute for InferenceMAX, the SemiAnalysis open-source benchmark tracking real-world inference performance across hardware and software stacks. As an independent research firm, SemiAnalysis rates against its own published methodology — its work with Nebius keeps benchmarks grounded in production-grade infrastructure.
