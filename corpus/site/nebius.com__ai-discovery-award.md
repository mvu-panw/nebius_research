## Phylo

Biomni is the first general-purpose biomedical AI agent. Biomni Lab is an Integrated Biology Environment providing a unified workspace where biologists can collaborate directly with AI agents to analyze complex datasets, query literature, design molecules, generate experimental protocols, and launch wet-lab experiments.

## Virgo

EndoDINO is an AI foundation model for endoscopic imaging trained on one of the largest endoscopy video datasets reported in the literature. It achieves state-of-the-art performance across GI endoscopy benchmarks and can predict ulcerative colitis treatment response from baseline colonoscopy videos.

## Decoy Therapeutics

D-MAVs (Designable Multi-Antivirals) are a new category of peptide antivirals engineered to target broadly conserved viral fusion mechanisms rather than specific viral proteins. D-MAVs function as “decoys” by anchoring into the host cell membrane and binding to conserved regions of the viral fusion machinery, effectively blocking infection at the earliest possible stage of cell entry.

## Honorable mentions

## DIOSynVax

DIOS Vaccines designs are based upon a foundational protein language model which is then fine tuned to represent the evolution of a viral family of interest. Because the model is trained on a vast amount of viral sequences, it generates novel sequences that cover a wide range of viral families. For this reason, the optimised sequences can be broadly effective on many different strains of a particular infectious disease.

## CytoReason

CytoReason’s Scientific Portfolio Management (SPM) platform leverages computational disease modeling to transform vast multimodal biomedical data into scalable, interpretable insights on the relationships between diseases, gene targets, drugs, and patient populations. It enables data-driven decision-making across the drug development lifecycle, supporting key applications such as target prioritization and indication expansion.

## Gero

ProtoBind is a sequence-first generative AI platform that bypasses 3D-structural bottlenecks to accelerate hit discovery for “undruggable” targets, delivering hit-finding precision comparable to leading structure-based solutions.

## ReasonBio, Inc

ReasonBio builds reasoning foundation models for molecular design. The flagship model, Rebio Preview, is the first protein design system that performs deliberative reasoning before generation, applying the same RL-trained reasoning paradigm behind models like o1 and Claude to biology.

## Synlico Inc.

Synlico’s product is CausalCell AI, a patient-native causal AI platform that converts large-scale primary patient single-cell data into a digital causal blueprint of disease biology. The platform is designed to answer the core therapeutic question: if we intervene on a gene, pathway, or drug input in a specific patient context, what functional change occurs downstream?

## Tahoe Therapeutics

Tahoe-100M is a giga-scale open-source single-cell perturbation atlas containing over 100 million transcriptomic profiles across thousands of drug–cell interactions. Built as foundational training data for next-generation AI “virtual cell” models, it is far larger than previously available public drug-perturbed single-cell datasets.

## Xelari Inc.

Xelari Platform is a computational system for designing functional DNA/RNA binders (Xelamers, the same functional class as aptamers, also called “chemical antibodies”) entirely in silico. It replaces traditional SELEX with a GPU-accelerated pipeline that moves from target protein to validated binder in up to 24 hours.

## FAQ

When can I submit my application?

The application deadline is on May 8, 2026.

Can I apply to more than one category?

No. You can only apply to one category.

Are there any special requirements for data used by startups?

We invite startups developing AI solutions in the fields of Life Sciences and Healthcare to participate. An important requirement is that any product operating with personal data must ensure this data is anonymized.

What does the consideration process look like?

Submission and initial review: Participants submit their applications online in written form, accompanied by additional materials that describe and validate the product’s functionality and results.

First consideration: Our in-house experts review the applications based on the specified criteria, considering the completeness of the information provided, breadth of functionality and publicly verified product outcomes. This assessment determines the finalists for each category.

Final jury evaluation: The full jury reviews and votes on the finalists to select the winners.

Winner selection: The jury’s evaluations, combined with the presentation scores, are summarized to determine the winners in each category.
