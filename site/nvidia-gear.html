InfiniBand fabric

  • Three-tier topology: a fabric root switch, then a POD (set of racks, joined by core switches), then a scalable unit (set of servers, joined by leaf switches). Topology-aware scheduling improves NCCL AllReduce by up to 20%. docs
  • Tenant isolation: each GPU cluster gets a unique InfiniBand P-Key inside a shared physical fabric. Clusters on the same fabric cannot talk over IB. docs
  • US fabrics: us-central1-a (H200), us-central1-b (B200), us-north1-a (B300). The new owned AI factories are not listed yet. Confirmed
  • The GPU fabric uses GPUDirect RDMA, with no CPU in the path. Nothing from PANW goes here.

Numbers that size the edge

  • Every Blackwell VM has a 400 Gbps BlueField-3 NIC on the VPC, carrying storage, internet and service traffic. H200 has 200 Gbps.
  • Summing those NICs overstates internet demand by orders of magnitude, because most VPC traffic is storage and east-west service traffic. Size the perimeter from DIA, peering and interconnect capacity per site, which is a discovery question. Inferred
  • Nebius already owns a BlueField-3 in every GPU host as its VPC data plane. Any DPU-hosted security, such as Prisma AIRS on BlueField, is a platform conversation with Nebius and not something a tenant can add. Inferred
  • Gigawatt campuses mean tens of thousands of GPU nodes per site. Even with a modest share of traffic going north-south, the edge needs terabit-class inspection. Inferred
