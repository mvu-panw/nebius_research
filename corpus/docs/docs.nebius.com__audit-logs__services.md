# Nebius AI Cloud services supported by Audit Logs

Audit Logs creates events for supported Nebius AI Cloud services and resources. Corresponding values of the `.service.name` field in [event JSONs](https://docs.nebius.com/audit-logs/events/reference.md) are given in parentheses:

* [Compute](https://docs.nebius.com/compute/index.md) (`COMPUTE`)
* [Compute Serial Console](https://docs.nebius.com/compute/virtual-machines/serial-console.md) (`COMPUTE_CONSOLE`)
* [Managed Service for Kubernetes®](https://docs.nebius.com/kubernetes/index.md) (`MK8S`)
* [Container Registry](https://docs.nebius.com/container-registry/index.md) (`REGISTRY`)
* [Serverless AI](https://docs.nebius.com/serverless/index.md) (`MSP_SERVERLESS`)
* [Managed Service for MLflow](https://docs.nebius.com/mlflow/index.md) (`MSP_MLFLOW`)
* [Managed Service for PostgreSQL®](https://docs.nebius.com/postgresql/index.md) (`MSP_POSTGRESQL`)
* [Object Storage](https://docs.nebius.com/object-storage/index.md) (`STORAGE`)
* [Data transfers](https://docs.nebius.com/object-storage/transfer/overview.md) in Object Storage (`DATA_TRANSFER`)
* [Virtual Networks](https://docs.nebius.com/vpc/index.md) (`VIRTUAL_NETWORKS`, `VPC` for logs older than June 23, 2025)
* [Nebius Tunnels](https://docs.nebius.com/tunnels/index.md) (`MARKETPLACE`)
* [Identity and Access Management resources](https://docs.nebius.com/iam/index.md) (`IAM`)
* [Key Management Service](https://docs.nebius.com/kms/index.md) (`KMS`)
* [SecretStash](https://docs.nebius.com/mysterybox/index.md) (`MBOX`)
