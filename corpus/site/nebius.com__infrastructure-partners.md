## A new model for building and operating AI infrastructure

Nebius partners with organizations that hold the capital, construction capability and regulatory standing to build and operate AI data centers in their markets.

Nebius provides the architecture and supply-chain access, deploys its full-stack AI cloud platform on the partner’s infrastructure, and takes the resulting capacity to market through its global sales organization. The partner funds and owns the facility and its hardware — including the GPUs — in full.

The partner’s data center joins the Nebius capacity pool and is offered to Nebius customers, who receive a single, consistent service whether they run on Nebius’ own infrastructure or a partner’s.

## Why partner with Nebius

## Own a world-class AI cloud

Build and own 100% of a purpose-built AI infrastructure asset — next-generation GPU clusters designed to Nebius standards, procured through Nebius’ supply-chain relationships, and running the same platform that powers Nebius’ own data centers.

## Operate without building the capability

No in-house cloud expertise required. Nebius deploys and maintains the full AI cloud stack, validates the clusters before launch, and equips your team to run the facility — through standard operating procedures, training and tooling, or Nebius-certified teams you engage directly.

## Monetize global AI demand

Demand for AI compute currently exceeds available supply. Your capacity is sold through Nebius’ global go-to-market organization from day one — and where you bring your own customers, that demand is served on the same platform.
