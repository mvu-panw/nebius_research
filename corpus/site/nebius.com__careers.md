## FAQ

I see numerous job openings on the website. Should I apply to all to boost my chances?

We’d advise you to apply only for roles that closely match your skills, knowledge, and experience.

Can I work remotely?

This largely depends on the team’s practices, but usually, working from home 1–2 days a week isn’t an issue. A few teams allow more remote work, but that’s not a common practice overall. Please let your recruiter know during the first call if this matters to you.

Will I undergo a probationary period?

Yes, there’s a probationary period lasting two or three months, depending on the specific country’s laws.

Why has the recruiter not gotten back to me with the results?

If it’s been over two weeks since you submitted your resume or had you last call, and you haven’t heard back, it’s our oversight. We aim to prevent such situations in out HR processes. If it does happen, please reach out to us at hr@nebius.com.

Do you hire interns or junior specialists?

Yes, we do. See our Early Talent Program page.

I’ve been approached by someone claiming to be a recruiter for Nebius. Is it genuine?

We’re aware of a number of cases of people posing as Nebius recruiters making approaches about potential jobs. Nebius recruiters will only contact you from @nebius.com email addresses, or via LinkedIn, where they are all identifiable and have a clear affiliation to the company. We do not cold-call people or use Gmail or other commercially available email for this. If you have concerns about an email you receive claiming to be from us, please contact hr@nebius.com

I have another question. How can I contact you?

Should you have any questions regarding the application process or benefits, please contact our HR team at hr@nebius.com.
