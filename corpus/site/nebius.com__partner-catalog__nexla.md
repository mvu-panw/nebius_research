# Nexla

Data

Nexla and Nebius take enterprises from fragmented data to production agents. Nexla converts enterprise data into governed, agent-ready data products and runs its multi-agent pipelines on Nebius AI Cloud inference infrastructure.

The partnership debuted at NVIDIA GTC 2026 with a multi-agent travel-planning system built with Tripadvisor — governed data products feeding agents served on Nebius.

See Nebius blog: From fragmented data to production-grade agents: Nebius, Nexla, and Tripadvisor at NVIDIA GTC.
