Nebius

"The AI cloud company". Account research and PANW positioning for technical enablement.

The 30-second version

  • Who: a full-stack AI cloud headquartered in Amsterdam and listed on Nasdaq (NBIS). It has 1,500+ staff and R&D hubs in Europe, the UK, North America and Israel. Founder and CEO is Arkady Volozh. It is an NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud on GB300 NVL72, and NVIDIA invested $2B in March 2026. about NVIDIA
  • Scale: Q2 2026 revenue was $582M (up 454% YoY), with AI cloud ARR of $3.0B and a 50% adjusted EBITDA margin. Contracted power is heading to 5 GW by year-end 2026, with more than 1 GW a year deployed from 2027. Q2 letter
  • The US build-out: 7 US sites, including two owned 1.2 GW AI factories (Independence, MO and Schuylkill County, PA) and an owned site in Birmingham, AL, all going live in 2027. Microsoft and Meta contracts are worth roughly $46B combined. US Strategy →
  • Incumbent: a mixed NGFW estate. A current Nebius job posting names "Palo Alto Networks, Check Point" and asks for Panorama and Strata Cloud Manager (plus FortiManager). Tenant-facing security is L3/L4 security groups plus a DIY IPsec VPN VM. job
  • Recommendation: PA-7500 at each owned campus edge, PA-5580 HA per dedicated customer block, PA-3440 for management, PA-415-5G for OOB, OT Security for the power plant and BMS, Prisma Access for field ops, and VM-Series as a tenant marketplace option. Nothing goes in the GPU fabric. details →
  • Why now: reference designs for the 2027 gigawatt campuses are being set now and reused at partner sites. Hyperscaler customers are financing the build and will want isolation evidence. Vera Rubin and BlueField-4 arrive in 2026–27.
