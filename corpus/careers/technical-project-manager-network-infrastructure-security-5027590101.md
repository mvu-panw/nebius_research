About Nebius:

Nebius is leading a new era in cloud infrastructure for the global AI economy. We are building a full-stack AI cloud platform that supports developers and enterprises from data and model training through to production deployment, without the cost and complexity of building large in-house AI/ML infrastructure.

Built by engineers, for engineers. From large-scale GPU orchestration to inference optimization, we own the hard problems across compute, storage, networking and applied AI.

The role

We are looking for a Technical Project Manager to join our Network Infrastructure team.You will bring network engineering and partner teams together to turn requirements into actionable plans and lead project delivery from planning through completion. Your work will span network deployments, internal network service improvements, and security initiatives across our global infrastructure, including offices and data centers. You will also help manage our network services as products, setting goals and metrics to guide improvements and measure how well they meet users’ needs.Your responsibilities will include:

- Lead networking and infrastructure projects across teams, agreeing on scope, priorities, owners, delivery commitments, and completion criteria.

- Turn incoming requests into actionable plans, clarify dependencies, and coordinate delivery with Global Networks, NetDev, SRE, and other partner teams.

- Support quarterly planning and day-to-day coordination by aligning priorities with engineering capacity and keeping tasks and dependencies on track.

- Manage risks and scope changes, follow through on commitments, help resolve blockers, and escalate issues when needed.

- Coordinate network deployments for offices and data centers, building shared deployment playbooks with clear milestones and responsibilities.

- Prepare and coordinate security initiatives within Network Infrastructure in partnership with the CISO, Platform Security, and Physical Security, balancing security work with other engineering priorities.

- Work with service owners and engineering teams to define service goals and metrics, prioritize improvements, and assess results against users’ needs.

- Keep project plans and status current in Jira, and provide concise updates on progress, blockers, risks, and decisions needed.

- Help teams adopt shared Network Infrastructure tools and practices, using simple, practical processes without unnecessary bureaucracy.

We expect you to have:

- Experience delivering networking projects involving multiple engineering teams, such as network deployments, connectivity upgrades, or migrations.

- A working understanding of networking and IT infrastructure concepts, sufficient to discuss requirements, dependencies, and trade-offs with engineers.

- Strong stakeholder management and negotiation skills, including the ability to resolve conflicting priorities and coordinate teams outside your reporting line.

- Experience managing scope, schedules, risks, dependencies, and engineering capacity across multiple concurrent projects.

- The ability to turn loosely defined requests into actionable plans with clear owners, milestones, and completion criteria.

- Familiarity with Agile, Kanban, and milestone-based planning, and the judgment to choose an approach that fits the work.

- Experience using Jira or similar tools to maintain project plans, coordinate work, and report on delivery.

- Clear written and verbal communication in English, including the ability to provide concise updates for technical teams and leadership.

- A practical approach to delivery: you take ownership, follow through, raise issues early, and help teams find workable solutions.

It would be a plus if you have:

- Experience translating security requirements into engineering plans and coordinating initiatives such as vulnerability remediation, network segmentation, firewall rollouts, or access management.

- Experience managing internal infrastructure services as products, including defining goals and metrics and prioritizing improvements based on users’ needs.

- Experience coordinating network deployments for office or data center launches.

- Experience developing reusable deployment playbooks or helping teams adopt shared tools and engineering practices.

- Hands-on experience configuring Jira workflows, building reports, or automating project processes.

- Relevant certifications, such as PMP, PRINCE2, or certifications in networking or security.
