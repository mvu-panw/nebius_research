# Outerbounds

Development

Outerbounds and Nebius have a technology partnership: the managed Metaflow platform runs on Nebius AI Cloud GPU compute, with Terraform deployment templates maintained in the Metaflow tooling.

Teams get Metaflow’s human-centric workflow authoring — born at Netflix — with training and inference capacity on Nebius underneath, moving from laptop prototypes to GPU-scale production without changing tools.
