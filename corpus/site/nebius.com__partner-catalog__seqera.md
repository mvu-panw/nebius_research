# Seqera

Orchestration

Seqera and Nebius jointly engineered a path for bioinformatics at GPU scale: Nextflow and nf-core pipelines run on Nebius AI Cloud through Slurm (Soperator) and Managed Kubernetes, with Seqera Platform available as a partner-managed application in the Nebius Marketplace.

Life-sciences teams orchestrate protein design, genomics, and drug-discovery pipelines with full traceability, on GPU clusters sized for the workload.
