# TD SYNNEX

Infrastructure

TD SYNNEX is a Nebius distribution partner, and its AI Infrastructure-as-a-Service offering is built on Nebius AI Cloud with dedicated NVIDIA HGX B300 clusters.

Through TD SYNNEX’s reseller network, customers procure dedicated Nebius GPU infrastructure through the channel they already buy from — bringing AI-native compute to the mainstream IT channel, with the reach of a distributor serving more than 150,000 customers worldwide.
