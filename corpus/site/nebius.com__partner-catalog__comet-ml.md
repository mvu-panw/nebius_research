GenAI & Agents

Comet’s Opik platform is available in the Nebius Marketplace as a partner-managed application on Nebius AI Cloud. Opik gives teams building agents and LLM applications on Nebius visibility into every step — prompts, retrieval, and tool calls — with LLM-as-a-judge evaluations and production monitoring.

Application support comes from Comet and infrastructure support from Nebius, so teams evaluate and monitor agents on the same cloud they train and serve on.
