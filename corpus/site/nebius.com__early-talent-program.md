Nebius is at an inflection point. The early days were defined by speed — bringing in senior talent to build and scale quickly. The next phase of sustainable growth requires something different: a deep, internal pipeline of people who grow with the company.

## Why choose Nebius’s Early Talent Program?

## Competitive salary

You’ll be compensated at a level comparable to a junior full-time employee, because you won’t just be watching from the sidelines. You’ll be doing hands-on work from day one.

## Real product ownership

No hypothetical assignments or throwaway tasks. As a part of the Early Talent Program, you’ll contribute directly to real products, infrastructure, and AI systems used by our customers and internal teams.

## Dedicated mentorship

Every Early Talent is paired with a buddy from their team, an experienced professional who supports your growth, gives regular feedback, and helps you navigate both the technical and professional aspects of the role.

## Experience Nebius from the inside

Get first-hand exposure to how Nebius operates: how teams collaborate, how decisions are made, and how large-scale AI and infrastructure products are built and scaled.

## Real impact

Your work matters. Early Talent will help shape the tools, platforms, and infrastructure powering the future of AI and get to see the tangible results of their contributions.

## FAQ

Do I need to be a university student to apply?

No. There are no formal age or degree requirements. Although we are primarily targeting graduates and final-year students, candidates outside this group may be considered based on skill level and assessment performance.

Do you offer visa support or relocation assistance?

Not at this stage. However, if there is sufficient demand, we may consider offering visa or relocation support in future Early Talent programs.

Can I work from a different location than where the position is based?

If you are legally authorized to work in the country where the role is based, this can be discussed. That said, we strongly encourage applying for positions in the location where you are currently based.

Is the Early Talent Program remote or office-based?

The program is office-based. While some flexibility is possible, we prioritise in-person work to ensure deep immersion and hands-on experience during the 2–3 months of the program.

What happens at the end of the program?

You’ll have a performance review with your mentor and manager. Based on how things go and current business needs, a full-time role may be offered.

What kind of projects will I work on?

Projects are team-specific and based on real work happening right now. You’ll contribute to active projects, not simulations or made-up tasks.

When is the application deadline?

The application deadline is May 31, 2026.

Can I request a different start or end date?

Yes. Start and end dates can be negotiated, depending on the team and location.
