## Project overview

The Highridge campus is designed, built, and operated entirely by Nebius. Located at the intersection of Interstate 81 and Gordon Mountain Road in Highridge Business Park, the facility will span approximately ~600 acres and deliver 1.2 GW of capacity upon full buildout, with an initial phase of 260 MW expected to be operational by October 2027.

## Reliable, Dedicated Power

- 

Nebius has secured a dedicated Energy Service Agreement with PPL Electric Utilities, including the engineering and construction of new energy infrastructure built exclusively for this campus

-

- 

PPL’s dedicated infrastructure ensures reliable, high-capacity energy delivery optimized for AI workloads

## Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Nebius and who are the project’s partners?

Nebius is a Nasdaq-listed technology company building next-generation AI cloud. By building and operating large-scale AI factories across the US and Europe, we provide essential infrastructure that delivers the most advanced compute.

Why Butler Township?

Butler Township, Schuylkill County sits at the intersection of power availability, deployment speed, and proximity to demand.

The area offers a compelling combination of infrastructure, workforce, and local leadership — including proactive zoning that permits data center development –– and we believe there is strong potential to create a long-term, mutually beneficial partnership with the community.

How large is the campus?

The campus within Highridge Business Park spans approximately ~600 acres. The facility is designed to deliver 1.2 GW of capacity upon full buildout, making it one of the largest private infrastructure investments in Pennsylvania’s history.

How large is the potential investment from Nebius?

The project is expected to contribute tens of millions of dollars annually to local, state, and corporate tax revenues from AI and GPU investments over the next five years.

What construction phase is the project currently in? How many jobs will be created through construction?

Highridge Business Park AI factory is currently in its first phase of development.

Power delivery is anticipated in phases, with 260 MW available by October 2027, 660 MW by January 2029, and 1,000+ MW by November 2029.

We anticipate creating significant construction employment during the buildout and permanent technical and operations roles upon completion. Facility construction will also boost job creation across local businesses in Butler Township and Schuylkill County.

When will the project be up and running?

The first phase is targeted to be operational by October 2027.

How will the project be powered? Will it impact local electric rates for consumers?

Nebius has secured a dedicated Energy Service Agreement with PPL Electric Utilities, which includes the engineering and construction of new energy infrastructure built specifically for this campus.

Power is secured and paid for entirely by Nebius, meaning the electricity is used only for our campus — without increasing residential electricity bills for residents.

Is there a back-up power plan in place?

The campus is designed to include a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) capable of providing backup and redundant power for approximately 30 minutes in the event of an outage.

Nebius is also exploring additional on-site mitigation strategies to further reduce any risk of service interruption.

How much water will this project use?

The facility’s closed-loop cooling system will continuously recycle water onsite, designed to minimize overall consumption.

The facility uses air-cooled and closed-loop systems in compliance with Butler Township’s zoning requirements.

Will the AI factory generate significant emissions?

The site will operate in full compliance with all local, state, and federal air quality standards, including strict Pennsylvania DEP requirements.

Will the AI factory generate excess noise?

The facility will be built with sound walls and buffering technology to minimize sound levels. The project will meet or operate below all local and state noise standards.

What safety protocols will be put in place at the facility?

The project will be designed and operated in full compliance with all applicable emergency preparedness and environmental protection processes to safeguard both the community and the environment.

Our team will work closely with municipal officials and engineering experts to ensure efficient planning and implementation of all emergency response procedures.

What are Nebius’ top priorities?

Nebius is committed to community engagement, with a focus on education and overall well-being in Butler Township.

As a global leader in artificial intelligence, Nebius brings access to advanced computing infrastructure and continued investment, helping Pennsylvania unlock new growth opportunities for years to come.

What commitments is Nebius prepared to make to the community?

Nebius is committed to being a responsible, long-term partner in the communities where we operate. We are dedicated to working closely with stakeholders throughout the planning, construction, and operation of our Butler Township campus to share our vision and discuss how we can be a strong community partner.

We believe that investing in training is essential to fostering innovation and preparing the next generation for emerging careers. Nebius plans to implement K–12 STEM programs to enhance AI literacy, as well as partner with local trade schools (such as the Schuylkill Technology Center) community colleges, and higher education across the region.
