The 30-second version

  • Shape of the build: Nebius started in the US with colocation (Kansas City, Nov 2024) and a leased build-to-suit (Vineland, NJ, for Microsoft). In 2026 it moved to owned gigawatt-scale AI factories in Missouri, Pennsylvania and Alabama. Owned capacity is now over 75% of contracted power. Confirmed Q1 letter
  • Timing: the owned US sites are in construction now and expected to be operational in 2027. Nebius plans to deploy more than 1 GW per year starting in 2027, and raised its year-end 2026 contracted power target to 5 GW. Confirmed Q2 letter
  • Demand: Microsoft is served from Vineland, and all Microsoft tranches had been delivered by August 2026. Meta's $27B Vera Rubin deal starts delivering in early 2027 "across multiple locations". Other Q2 wins include a US AI neolab and a large US quant trading firm. Confirmed Meta release
  • Why it matters for PANW: every owned campus is a greenfield edge, management and OOB build that Nebius designs and buys itself. A colo landlord doesn't make those calls. Network design for the 2027 sites is being decided now. Inferred details ↓

  • Leadership: Dan Lawrence became SVP and GM for the Americas in March 2026. He is Boston-based and came from Akamai cloud sales and AWS, and is building out US teams "as Nebius adds gigawatt-scale capacity". Confirmed release
  • Offices: San Francisco and Dallas (since September 2024) and New York. Roles are also posted in Palo Alto and Austin, and the Eigen AI and Clarifai acquisitions added a Bay Area engineering presence. Confirmed newsroom

  • AI-native and enterprise: Reflection, Cohere, a US AI neolab, a large US quant trading firm and AMI (Yann LeCun). Confirmed Q2 letter
  • Sovereign: Palantir named Nebius its preferred sovereign AI infrastructure partner in September 2026, bringing Nebius compute "inside the Palantir enterprise perimeter". Confirmed release
  • Asset-light model (July 2026): partners build data centers to Nebius reference designs, and Nebius runs its software and handles go-to-market on them. Expect more US capacity on partner sites. Confirmed release

What it means for PANW Inferred

  • Owned sites change who buys. At Kansas City, Minnesota and Oklahoma, Nebius is a tenant, and the landlord owns the building, BMS and physical plant. At Independence, Pennsylvania and Birmingham, Nebius owns the whole stack. That means a new perimeter, management network, OOB network and OT/BMS network at each site, with designs for 2027 go-lives being locked in during late 2026 and H1 2027.
  • PANW is already a known quantity. Nebius's current Network Security Engineer posting asks for "Palo Alto Networks, Check Point, or similar" NGFWs, User-ID, Entra ID and Okta integration, ZTNA/SASE, and "Panorama, Strata Cloud Manager, and FortiManager". It also covers "large hyper scale DC security solutions: IPS/NDR, AntiDDoS". The estate is mixed, but Palo Alto is named first. Ask who owns the DC edge standard.
  • Hyperscaler tenants raise the bar. Microsoft and Meta capacity is dedicated, so expect customer-driven requirements for isolation, logging and attestation at the edge of each dedicated block. A per-tenant HA pair, as in the Lambda Private Cloud model, maps cleanly onto this. See Firewall Positioning.
  • Power is OT. Vineland runs on-site Bloom fuel cells, Nebius signed a company-wide Bloom deal in May 2026, and Independence is tied to a municipal utility. Fuel cells, switchgear and BMS form an OT attack surface. Position OT security and segmentation alongside the data center firewalls.
  • Sovereign and regulated demand: Palantir's "inside the perimeter" partnership and the US quant trading firm both bring compliance-driven buyers for whom Nebius's security story becomes part of the sale.
  • Who to find: the Americas GM's org (Dan Lawrence), the network infrastructure engineering manager for the US (open role), the US data center facilities manager (Philadelphia), and the security architecture team, which is hiring in Tel Aviv and Europe.
