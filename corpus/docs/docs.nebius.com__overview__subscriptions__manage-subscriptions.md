# How to manage subscriptions in Nebius AI Cloud

## How to subscribe to notifications

To subscribe, you can use [contacts](https://docs.nebius.com/overview/contacts/index.md) that you [added earlier](https://docs.nebius.com/overview/contacts/manage-contacts.md#how-to-create-a-contact), or you can add new contacts when you set up the subscription.

To subscribe to notifications:

**Web console**

1. In the sidebar, go to  **Administration** → **Notification settings**.

2. Choose a subscription type:

   * **Tenant**: Notifications about projects and resources in your tenant.
   * **Billing**: Notifications about invoices, payment deadlines and account balance updates.
   * **Support**: Notifications about your support tickets.

   See also [Subscriptions in Nebius AI Cloud](https://docs.nebius.com/overview/subscriptions/index.md).

3. Click **Add email**.

4. In the window that opens, specify a new contact.

If you want to add a new contact, verify it by using the code that the service sends to this contact.

## How to unsubscribe from notifications

**Web console**

1. In the sidebar, go to  **Administration** → **Notification settings**.
2. Choose a subscription and then click the  icon next to the contact that you want to remove.

Notification emails that you receive also have a link to unsubscribe.

> [!WARNING]
>
> You cannot unsubscribe from [tenant and billing notifications](https://docs.nebius.com/overview/subscriptions/index.md) if nobody else in your tenant receives them. Ask someone in your tenant to subscribe before you remove your contacts.
