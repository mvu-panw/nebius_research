b) Demonstrated initiative: applicants must already be actively building and sharing AI-related work, whether through open-source contributions, content creation, or community engagement.

a) Produce one to two content pieces (e.g., blog posts, tutorials, videos) OR one open-source software project OR one event or workshop.
