## FAQ

What is the difference between Certifications and other credentials?

Nebius Certifications validate your skills in AI cloud technologies and demonstrate your ability to perform specific job tasks using Nebius products and services. These certifications are recognized by our partners, customers, and Nebius itself. To earn a certification, you must pass an exam.

Nebius Academy also provides a wide range of training programs focused on different AI domains, not necessarily related to Nebius products. Learners can earn achievements and digital badges for completing courses or developing specific skills. These are usually granted without a formal exam.

You can earn both — they complement each other nicely!

What is the exam format?

All exams are conducted online through our exam partner’s platform.

They are remotely proctored using AI-assisted monitoring, which records your session and ensures exam rules are followed. During the exam, you won’t be able to use unauthorized devices or materials.

You’ll need to install a secure browser provided by our exam partner to take the test.

How can I share my credentials?

Nebius certifications are a great way to showcase your expertise and commitment to learning.

Once you earn your certification, you can share it on LinkedIn and add it to the “Licenses & Certifications” section of your profile. You can also showcase your badge in your Credly wallet alongside your other achievements.

You can also download and print your digital certificate for your records.

How long is the exam, and what types of questions can I expect?

The number of questions may vary as exams are regularly updated to reflect changes in technology and job roles.

Most exams include 30–50 questions in multiple-choice and multiple-response formats, combining knowledge-based and practical, role-focused scenarios.

Exam duration is up to 1 hour.

How much do exams cost?

Our goal is not to profit from certification exams, but to maintain a high-quality certification standard.

Exam fees vary by certification and may change over time. Please check the individual exam pages for the latest pricing information.

We also offer discounts for:

- Early registrations

- Partners and integrators

- Existing customers

- Educational institutions

For discount inquiries, contact us at [academy@nebius.com](mailto: academy@nebius.com).

Who is eligible to take a Nebius certification exam?

Anyone aged 18 or older is eligible to take a Nebius certification exam. There are currently no formal prerequisites.

However, each certification is designed for specific roles and skill levels. Some exams may require hands-on experience with cloud technologies and Nebius AI Cloud products.

We recommend reviewing the exam guide for your chosen certification to understand the expected knowledge and recommended experience.

How long is the certification valid?

Nebius certifications are valid for 3 years. Each certificate includes the date of issuance and the expiration date.

Once your certification expires, you can retake the exam to renew your credential.

Even after expiration, your digital badge will remain visible in your Credly badge wallet as proof that you successfully earned the certification.

Can I retake the exam if I don’t pass?

Yes. If you don’t pass, you can retake the exam after 1 day. Retakes follow the same process:

- The exam fee applies

- You can use a promo code if available
