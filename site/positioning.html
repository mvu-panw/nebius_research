Why not the alternatives

Prisma AIRS on BlueField

Nebius already uses BlueField-3 as the VPC NIC in every Blackwell host, so the DPU is Nebius's data plane and not a free slot. AIRS on BlueField is designed for roughly 1–5 Gbps per DPU, which fits inference endpoints (Token Factory) and not training. Treat it as a platform roadmap conversation for Vera Rubin and BlueField-4, not a near-term sale.

Anything in the GPU fabric

Nebius isolates GPU clusters on shared InfiniBand fabrics with P-Keys and runs GPUDirect RDMA at 3.2–6.4 Tb/s per node. Nothing inline belongs there. Say this first, unprompted. It shows we understand AI infrastructure and keeps the conversation on north-south, where we win.

Talk track

Gigawatt-scale, performance-first narrative

  1. "We never touch your GPU fabric." Every enforcement point is north-south. P-Key isolation and RDMA stay exactly as designed.
  2. "Your edge grows in phases, and so does ours." Pennsylvania goes from 260 MW to 660 MW to 1 GW+ over 2027–2029. A PA-7500 adds threat-prevention capacity card by card, so there's no forklift upgrade in each phase.
  3. "Prove isolation to the customers who are financing you." Microsoft and Meta commitments back Nebius's secured debt ($775M in July, $3.5B Citi in October). A dedicated, logged NGFW edge per block is evidence those buyers recognize.
  4. "One OS from campus edge to field laptop." PA-7500, PA-5500, PA-3400, PA-415-5G and Prisma Access all run under Strata Cloud Manager. Nebius is already hiring for that skillset.
  5. "Sell security, don't just buy it." Nebius's VPC security is L3/L4 security groups and a DIY IPsec VM. VM-Series in the marketplace, and later a managed tier, helps Nebius win regulated enterprise and sovereign (Palantir) workloads.

Objection handling

  • "Firewalls slow down training." Agreed, which is why none go in the training path. We size only the internet, peering and interconnect edge, at line rate with threat prevention on.
  • "We already standardized on Check Point / Fortinet." The job post lists Palo Alto first and asks for Panorama and SCM, so the skills are already in-house. The new owned US campuses are greenfield, so they are the natural point to standardize on a security-on throughput number rather than a firewall-only number.
  • "Last time we added firewalls, us-central1 went down." This is true, and it's public: on September 3, 2025, "new Firewall nodes onboarding" between Nebius's Backbone and DC routing domains triggered a 1h45m routing loop (post-mortem). Raise it before they do. Lead with an insertion design that keeps the firewall out of route recomputation (vwire/L2, or a tightly scoped BGP role), staged validation, and HA that fails without perturbing routing. Bring a PANW DC architect. Inferred
  • "Hyperscaler customers bring their own security." Inside their tenancy, yes. The facility edge, management plane and OT remain Nebius's responsibility and Nebius's liability.
  • "We're moving too fast to redesign." Nebius plans to deploy more than 1 GW a year from 2027, using reference designs that are also handed to asset-light partners. Get PANW into the reference design once and it replicates to every owned and partner site.
  • "Sovereign deals need something different." Palantir's "inside the perimeter" partnership and possible US government work point to on-prem PA-Series under Panorama with offline updates. Bring in the PANW federal team. Nebius's trust center does not list FedRAMP.
