# Taxes

Depending on your [payer type](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/payers.md) and location, using Nebius AI Cloud resources might be subject to taxes. In some countries, reverse charge may apply to companies that provide a valid VAT ID or GSTIN (A tax ID identifies your company for general tax purposes, while a value-added tax (VAT) ID confirms that your company is also registered to handle VAT or goods-and-services tax (GST).) in their [billing setup](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/sign-up.md#set-up-billing).

If taxes are applicable, they are included on your [invoices](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/invoices.md#invoices) on a separate line.

The table below outlines the taxes applicable to companies and individuals in different locations. If your location isn't listed, Nebius doesn't add taxes to your invoices.
