# Zenvue

Infrastructure

Zenvue is a Nebius partner serving the Middle East and Europe, offering migration assessments, architecture, post-training support, and managed services for Nebius AI Cloud.

Zenvue helps enterprises move AI workloads onto Nebius — including migrations from proprietary model APIs to open models on Nebius — and operates them day to day.
