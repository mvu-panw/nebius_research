# Why liquid cooling is the hottest new trend in AI data centers

AI growth is pushing data centers to their thermal limits. In this whitepaper, published in August 2025, we explain why liquid cooling is now a priority for high-density AI clusters

The world of work is fundamentally changing as AI integrates into every aspect of the economy. A recent McKinsey survey shows that 67% of organizations plan to increase their AI investments in the coming year. As AI workloads increase in magnitude, they require more powerful GPUs.

Sophisticated cooling systems have become critical to keep AI hardware functioning at its best. Explore traditional and modern data center cooling technologies, identify key trends for an AI-powered future and discover what they mean for power savings, higher rack density and sustainability.
