# Terms and Conditions of the AI Discovery Award

The AI Discovery Award (the “Award”) is organized by Nebius B.V. (“Nebius”) to recognize and reward innovative AI products in the Life Sciences and Healthcare industry. The Award aims to increase awareness, engage with high-potential startups, and position Nebius as a provider with profound industry expertise.

## 1. Eligibility criteria for Participants1. Eligibility criteria for Participants

To participate in the Award, startups must meet the following requirements:

a) Develop AI products in fields such as drug discovery, biotech, genomics, and healthtech.

b) Be at stage from pre-seed up to and including Series C round or self-funded.

c) Have a legal entity, a working website, a product Minimum Viable Product (MVP), and a Go-to-Market (GTM) strategy.

Nebius reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to refuse or reject any company’s participation in the Award for any reason, including but not limited to failure to meet the eligibility criteria, incomplete or inaccurate application, or any other reason that Nebius deems fit. Nebius’s decision in this regard shall be final and binding.

Startups admitted by Nebius for the participation in the Award, shall be referred to hereinafter as “Participant” or “Participants”.

## 3. Evaluation Criteria3. Evaluation Criteria

The Award will be evaluated based on the following criteria, each with equal weight (20%):

a) AI Powered: The product uses advanced AI/ML technologies for model training or inference and manages High-Performance Computing (HPC) workloads effectively.

b) Technological Innovation: The product or service advances the industry by introducing new capabilities or significant improvements.

c) Functionality & Advantages: The product offers superior or unique functionality, providing a competitive edge and enhancing business processes.

d) Performance & Efficiency: The product delivers measurable results with clear metrics or case studies, improving customer performance effectively.

e) Global Impact: The product addresses unique needs and solves specific problems, creating meaningful value on a global scale.

f) Financial Impact & Cost Effectiveness: The product generates new revenue streams and reduces costs, ensuring a clear, measurable return on investment, driving financial sustainability in healthcare and life sciences.

Each member of the jury will assess the participants based on these criteria using a 10-point scale.

## 4. Jury Panel4. Jury Panel

The Award will be judged by a panel of at least four experts (“Jury Panel”), consisting of:

a) Two recognized experts from the Life Sciences and Healthcare industry, invited by Nebius to participate in the jury panel.

b) Two representatives from Nebius, who will provide expertise on the company’s industry knowledge and requirements.

The Jury Panel will be responsible for evaluating the participants and selecting the winners based on the evaluation criteria.
