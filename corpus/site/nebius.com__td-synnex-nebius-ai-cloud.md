## 

Nebius and TD SYNNEX Launch TD SYNNEX x Nebius AI Cloud

Nebius and TD SYNNEX have partnered to launch the TD SYNNEX x Nebius AI Cloud, powered by a dedicated NVIDIA HGX™ B300 GPU on Nebius operated infrastructure

The deployment marks the first time a global IT distributor has reserved AI Factory-grade infrastructure from an AI-native cloud provider. Through the TD SYNNEX x Nebius AI Cloud, TD SYNNEX partners can combine Nebius infrastructure with NVIDIA AI software, ISV platforms, and services to design, validate, and deploy enterprise AI solutions on guaranteed GPU capacity.
