## FAQ

When do applications close?

Applications for the 2026 program close October 25, 2026.

Can I apply to more than one category?

Yes. Each application should focus on a single product or service that your startup provides. During the application you’ll select the specific category you’re nominating that product for.

You may submit the same product for multiple categories, or multiple products for the same category. While there is no limit to the number of applications you can submit, we recommend focusing on your strongest cases.

Who can apply for the program?

Applications are open to Physical AI founders that meet the following criteria:

• Minimum $1M raised

• Core product must have a clear Physical AI use case

• Nominated product must align with at least one of the five award categories

• Registered legal entity

• Live public website

• Demonstrable MVP in active use or testing

View the full Terms & Conditions for more details.

What does the consideration process look like?

All applications are screened by our in-house experts for overall quality and completeness. Incomplete or unclear applications may be filtered out.

A panel of judges will review the remaining applications, scoring each on depth of innovation, breadth of implementation, demonstrated market potential, and the realized impact of the technology to determine the finalists for each category.

Finalists will be voted on by a jury to determine the winners for each category.

Who should I contact with questions about my submission?

Contact startups@nebius.com with any questions about your submission.
