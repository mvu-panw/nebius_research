# Dstack

Infrastructure
Orchestration

dstack provides ML teams with a unified control plane for GPU provisioning and orchestration across cloud, Kubernetes, and on prem. It streamlines development, training, and inference — reducing costs 3–7x and preventing lock in.

dstack integrates natively with Nebius AI Cloud: point dstack at a Nebius service account and it provisions GPU compute directly for training, inference, and dev environments.

It is also available in the Nebius Marketplace as a free, self-managed Kubernetes deployment. Teams get open-source orchestration (MPL 2.0) with Nebius price-performance underneath.
