## FAQ

Are the courses free?

Yes. Agentic AI Builder and Fundamentals of Deep Learning are free to take on Nebius Academy.

Members of the Nebius AI Builder Program also receive a credit bundle from 11 partners for hands-on work.

What is the difference between the co-developed courses and the open-source NVIDIA DLI courses?

Co-developed courses are designed and taught by Nebius and NVIDIA engineers and run on Nebius products such as Nebius Token Factory.

Open-source NVIDIA DLI courses are NVIDIA’s own curriculum, published under an open license and hosted on Nebius Academy with attribution to the NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute.

Will I get a certificate?

Completing Agentic AI Builder earns a Credly digital badge you can add to LinkedIn. The course also prepares you for the Agentic AI Builder certification exam, which is a separate, proctored exam with its own official certificate.

The NVIDIA DLI workshop at GTC Berlin prepares you for NVIDIA’s associate-level AI Model and Application Development certification.

Beyond these courses, Nebius Academy runs a full certification program — AI Leader, AI CloudOps Engineer, and Agentic AI Builder are live, with AI Infrastructure Engineer and AI Solutions Architect on the way. See all Nebius certifications.

Do I need my own GPUs?

No. Agentic AI Builder runs on Nebius Token Factory, and both GTC Berlin workshops run on Nebius AI Cloud.

Can I still get into the NVIDIA DLI workshop at GTC Berlin?

The 20 October workshop is sold out. Join the waitlist to be notified if a seat opens.

Registration for the Nebius workshop on 16 October is open, subject to host approval.

Can I bring a DLI course to my team or university?

Write to academy@nebius.com. Nebius Academy runs programs for companies and universities, and we can advise on which courses fit.
