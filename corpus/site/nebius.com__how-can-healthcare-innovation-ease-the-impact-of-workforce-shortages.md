# How can healthcare innovation ease the impact of workforce shortages?

Healthcare and technology leaders discussed in a roundtable hosted by Nebius, AstraZeneca, and NVIDIA, how AI can help health systems address challenges posed by workforce deficits worldwide.

The World Health Organisation (WHO) projects the global shortfall to exceed 11 million by 2030. Experts explored how AI can alleviate daily pressures managed by medical professionals and amplify evidence-based care delivery.

This whitepaper presents their insights on:

- 

How AI can help clinical staff enhance patient prioritization, scale follow-up capacity, ease administrative burdens, and enable new therapies.

- 

Success factors for clinical implementation, including human-centric adoption strategies, integrated systems, regulatory clarity, and patient-oriented incentives.

- 

Partnership opportunities for hospitals, tech companies, pharmaceutical leaders, and policymakers to find solutions with clinical realities and patient needs.
