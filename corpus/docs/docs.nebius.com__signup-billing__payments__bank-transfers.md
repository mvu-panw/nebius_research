# How to pay by bank transfer

[Companies](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/payers.md) can pay for Nebius AI Cloud services by bank transfer. To pay by bank transfer, [set them as your payment method](#setting-up-bank-transfers); the Nebius sales team will help you set it up. After that, you will be able to pay for your [pay-as-you-go](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/billing-models/payg.md) and [commitment discounts](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/billing-models/committed-usage.md) following the [payment process](#paying-by-bank-transfer).

Bank transfers are not available to individuals. For companies from Israel, bank transfers in Israeli shekels (ILS, ₪) are the only payment method available.

## Setting up bank transfers

During the signup, contact the sales team and request this option. You can also switch to this payment method by [contacting support](https://console.nebius.com/support/create-ticket).

## Paying by bank transfer

After bank transfers are set up as your payment method, you will be able to use them to pay your [invoices](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/invoices.md#invoices) and [proforma invoices](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/invoices.md#proforma-invoices). Nebius issues them depending on your [billing model](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/billing-models/overview.md) and payment type:

* ***Pay-as-you-go usage***

  At the beginning of each month you will receive an [invoice](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/invoices.md#invoices) for the previous month's usage. This includes details of the resources used and their total cost, as well as bank transfer details.

* ***Commitments***

  Generally, commitments are prepaid. At the beginning of the commitment period you will receive a [proforma invoice](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/invoices.md#proforma-invoices) with bank transfer details. After you have paid with the proforma invoice, the Nebius team will process it and send you an [invoice](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/invoices.md#invoices) as a record of what you have paid for. You do not need to pay an invoice once you have paid a proforma invoice.

  For different arrangements, please contact the Nebius sales team.

* ***PAYG balance top-up***

  To top up your PAYG balance, request a [proforma invoice](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/invoices.md#proforma-invoices) from the Nebius sales team. Once you have paid the proforma invoice, the Nebius team will process it and send you an [invoice](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/invoices.md#invoices) as a record of what you have paid for. You do not need to pay this invoice once you have paid a proforma invoice. You can also check your balance in the [Billing](https://console.nebius.com/billing) section of the web console.

You will get proforma invoices and invoices at the email address that you specified in the contract application.

> [!WARNING]
>
> If you make a mistake during the transfer, we might not be able to find and apply your payment. Please double-check all the details. If your Nebius AI Cloud resources remain unpaid, your account may be suspended.

## Changing payment method

To change the payment method to [bank card](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/card.md), [contact support](https://console.nebius.com/support/create-ticket).
