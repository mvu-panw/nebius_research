# Audit Logs

With Audit Logs in Nebius AI Cloud, you can see who did what and when with your resources to ensure security, compliance and accountability.

The service is [in preview](https://docs.nebius.com/overview/services.md#service-and-application-stages) and is available in all [Nebius AI Cloud regions](https://docs.nebius.com/overview/regions.md).
