# Milestone

GenAI & Agents
Development

Milestone builds its Hafnia computer-vision platform on Nebius AI Cloud, fine-tuning vision-language models — including NVIDIA Cosmos Reason — on curated, compliant video data.

Training on Nebius gives Milestone the GPU capacity and EU data sovereignty that video AI demands, with hands-on engineering support along the way.

The work is part of the Nebius and NVIDIA collaboration on physical AI, and powers vision AI used in cities, transportation, and security worldwide.

See Nebius announcement featuring Milestone: Nebius teams with NVIDIA to build cloud for robotics and physical AI.
