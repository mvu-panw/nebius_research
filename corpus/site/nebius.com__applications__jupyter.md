by Project Jupyter

Jupyter provides interactive computing environments for data science, AI, and ML. This product offers two deployment modes: JupyterLab for single-user notebook workflows with PyTorch and CUDA support, and JupyterHub for multi-user collaborative environments on Kubernetes. Both include GPU acceleration, making them ideal for training, fine-tuning, and experimentation without manual infrastructure setup.Deploy on Nebius
