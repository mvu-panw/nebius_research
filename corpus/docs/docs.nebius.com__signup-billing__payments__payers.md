# Types of payers

There are two types of payers in Nebius AI Cloud.

| Type | Individual | Company |
| - | - | - |
| Payment models | [PAYG](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/billing-models/payg.md) | [PAYG](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/billing-models/payg.md) and [commitment discounts](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/billing-models/committed-usage.md) |
| Payment methods | [Bank card](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/card.md) | [Bank card](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/card.md) (except Israel), [bank transfers](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/bank-transfers.md) |
| Currency | USD | USD (except Israel), ILS (Israel) |
| Payment time | At the beginning of the month | At the beginning of the month for PAYG, depends on the contract details for commitments |
| Payment documents | [Invoices](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/invoices.md#invoices) | [Proforma invoices](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/invoices.md#proforma-invoices), [invoices](https://docs.nebius.com/signup-billing/payments/invoices.md#invoices) |
| Activation | Automatic after adding a card and paying the initial \$25 | Automatic after adding a card and paying the initial \$25 (except Israel), or manual through the Nebius team |

You choose a payer type during the signup. To change the account type, [contact support](https://console.nebius.com/support/create-ticket).
