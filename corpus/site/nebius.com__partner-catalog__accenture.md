# Accenture

GenAI & Agents
Infrastructure

Accenture and Nebius help enterprises move AI initiatives from pilot to production. Accenture brings its consulting practice and industry depth; Nebius provides the AI-native infrastructure those programs run on — dedicated GPU clusters, managed Kubernetes, and Slurm environments.

The companies have presented together at NVIDIA GTC on moving from pilots to production without compromise (see on demand), and work jointly with enterprise and public-sector organizations building on accelerated computing.

For customers, the pairing means a single path from AI strategy through deployment, on infrastructure purpose-built for training and inference at scale.
