# Datadog

Observability

Nebius AI Cloud logs are available in Datadog through a supported integration. Teams standardized on Datadog can collect logs from Nebius services — virtual machines, Managed Kubernetes, managed MLflow and PostgreSQL, and container jobs — with out-of-the-box dashboards and monitor templates.

Nebius AI Cloud ships with its own observability tooling; the Datadog integration adds choice for organizations that centralize monitoring in Datadog.

See Datadog’s blog: Monitor Nebius AI Cloud with Datadog.
