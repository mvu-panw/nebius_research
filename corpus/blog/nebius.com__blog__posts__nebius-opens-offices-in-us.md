# Nebius opens offices in the US

Source: https://nebius.com/blog/posts/nebius-opens-offices-in-us

Skip to main content

We are putting ourselves on the map in the United States.

Since taking our first steps in America, we’ve understood that the demand for AI-native cloud services in the US market is unprecedented. Growing our presence in the US means we can be closer to our customers and support innovative American AI businesses on their journey into the future.

As well as actively hiring in the US, we now also have customer-facing hubs where we can meet our clients and partners and where Nebius employees can work in environments that foster creativity. Our first base is located in the iconic Ferry Building in San Francisco. We also have a beautiful space on Dallas Parkway in Texas — not that far from the HQ of our sister company Avride in Austin. Our third office will open in New York later this year.
