## Key features

### End-to-end AI observability

Observe every layer of your AI stack. Trace every prompt, model invocation, agent interaction, downstream service call, and infrastructure dependencies to understand behavior and impact in real time.

### Model, agent, and cost insights

Monitor model latency, token usage, error rates, GPU and compute utilization, and agent workflows to optimize performance, reliability, and cost as AI workloads scale.

### Production grade quality, governance, and scale

Safely scale agentic AI with proactive alerts, guardrail analysis, token optimization, A/B testing and model versioning along with continuous quality evaluations against live production data to track drift over time, analyze guardrails, and maintain full data lineage and audit trails to support governance, risk management, and regulatory requirements.

### Intelligent troubleshooting and root cause analysis

Pinpoint the exact prompt, tool, retrieval step, or service causing latency, errors, or bottlenecks using deep drill-downs so teams can debug AI apps faster and resolve issues before they impact users.
