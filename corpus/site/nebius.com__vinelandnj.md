## Project overview

The facility is a build-to-suit for Nebius by DataOne, with construction led by Vineland-based Northeast Precast. Nebius will serve as the tenant, installing and managing the server and GPU infrastructure that powers advanced AI capabilities for leading U.S. companies and research institutions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Nebius and who are the project’s partners?

Nebius is a Nasdaq-listed technology company building next-generation AI cloud. By building and operating large-scale AI factories across the US and Europe, we provide essential infrastructure that delivers the most advanced compute.

The project partners each bring experience in large-scale AI infrastructure. The AI factory is designed for Nebius, operated and owned by DataOne, and built locally by Northeast Precast. Nebius will be the facility’s tenant, operating server racks and GPU clusters that service our clients’ advanced AI needs.

Why Vineland?

Vineland sits at the intersection of power availability, deployment speed and proximity to demand, which is why Nebius sees clear opportunities where Vineland can play a leading role in the AI age. The city offers a promising combination of infrastructure, workforce, and leadership, and we believe there is strong potential to create a long-term, mutually beneficial partnership.

How large is the potential investment from Nebius?

DataOne will contribute millions of dollars in local tax revenue and is expected to become one of the largest taxpayers in South Jersey, directly supporting local communities, public services, and infrastructure.

Is the facility supported by financial incentives such as tax abatements?

The Vineland City Council and mayor approved an ordinance in January 2026, authorizing Data-One a five-year payment in lieu of taxes (PILOT) agreement, an annual service charge, to provide tax relief for the Vineland facility and encourage development.

What construction phase is the project currently in? How many jobs will be created through construction?

Construction remains ongoing at the site, and union and non-union partnerships prioritize local businesses, creating roughly 1,000 highly skilled, technical construction jobs and roughly 200 permanent on-site jobs. Facility construction is expected to boost job creation across local small businesses in Vineland, such as restaurants and hotels, with 90% of employees local to the area.

Nebius' Vineland facility meets New Jersey’s data center requirements.

When will the project be up and running?

The AI factory is expected to be operational by 2027.

How will the project be powered? Will it impact local electric rates for consumers?

We bring in and pay for our own power. We partnered with Bloom Energy to power the project with modular fuel cells installed onsite. This means the electricity is generated and used only for our AI factory, without increasing residential electricity bills. Unlike traditional power sources, fuel cells produce electricity without burning fuel, making them a cleaner, more sustainable energy solution.

What is Bloom Energy?

Bloom Energy develops and manufactures fuel cells that generate electricity on-site for its customers, without combustion. Bloom Energy’s fuel cells have been operating in commercial deployment since 2008 and are used by companies, hospitals, college campuses, and utilities across the United States and internationally. From day one, Bloom Energy’s mission has been to make clean, reliable energy affordable for everyone in the world. To find out more about Bloom Energy we invite you to visit Bloom Energy’s website.

What is a fuel cell?

Fuel cells have been around since the 1800s and have powered hospitals, college campuses, and data centers across the U.S. for nearly two decades. They are not new or experimental. What makes them different from a traditional power plant is that they generate electricity without burning anything. Instead of combustion, oxygen from the air combines with a fuel source inside the cell to produce electricity through a controlled electrochemical process. No burning means cleaner air, far less water use, and a much quieter operation.

How much water will this project use?

The facility’s closed-loop cooling system will continuously recycle water onsite. The project will require a one-time fill of 1 million gallons of water, and an annual consumption equivalent to approximately 7 Olympic swimming pools mainly for typical office building use.

Will the AI factory generate significant emissions?

The project is classified as a minor emissions source, with power provided by Bloom Energy’s fuel cell technology. The site will operate and adhere to all local, state and federal air quality standards.

Will the AI factory generate excess noise?

The facility is designed to operate at or below noise ordinance levels. This is achieved through combination of fuel cell power, placing the air chillers inside, and using various sound buffering techniques, including acoustical.

What safety protocols will be put in place at the facility?

The project will be designed and operated in full compliance with all applicable emergency preparedness and environmental protection processes to safeguard both the community and the environment.

Our team will work closely with municipal officials and engineering experts to ensure efficient planning and implementation of all emergency response procedures.

What are Nebius’ top priorities?

Nebius is committed to community engagement, with a focus on education and overall well-being in Vineland. As a global leader in artificial intelligence, Nebius brings access to advanced computing infrastructure and continued investment, helping advance the Einstein Corridor in New Jersey.

What commitments is Nebius prepared to make to the community?

Nebius is committed to being a responsible, long-term partner in the communities where we operate. We are dedicated to working closely with stakeholders throughout the planning, construction, and operation of our planned Vineland facility to share our vision and discuss how we can be a strong community partner.

We believe that investing in training is essential to fostering innovation and in preparing the next generation of emerging careers. Nebius plans to implement K-12 STEM programs to enhance AI literacy, as well as partner with local trade schools and community colleges across South Jersey, including Rowan University to advance education initiatives. In addition, Nebius will serve the Vineland community through non-profit conservation and volunteer efforts, as well as initiatives to support first responders and emergency training preparedness.
