# CrowdStrike

Infrastructure

CrowdStrike (NASDAQ: CRWD), a global cybersecurity leader, has redefined modern security with the world’s most advanced cloud-native platform for protecting critical areas of enterprise risk — endpoints and cloud workloads, identity and data.

CrowdStrike and Nebius are bringing the CrowdStrike Falcon platform to Nebius AI Cloud, giving customers unified visibility and AI-powered detection and response across their AI infrastructure and runtime environments.

Security teams standardized on Falcon can protect training and inference workloads on Nebius with the platform they already run across the rest of their estate — extending established security operations to AI infrastructure.
