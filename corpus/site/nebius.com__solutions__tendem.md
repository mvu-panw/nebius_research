## Tendem by Toloka: a new model for reliable AI

AI agents can fail when they encounter ambiguity, edge cases, or decisions that require accountable judgment. Tendem introduces a structured way to extend agent capabilities with verified human judgment. Rather than relying on manual review processes or disconnected human interventions, Tendem enables AI systems to escalate ambiguity to verified domain experts via Model Context Protocol (MCP).

Tendem handles:

- Routing to the appropriate expert from Toloka’s vetted network

- Coordinating AI + human task execution

- Applying quality control

- Returning production-ready, structured results

This approach embeds human validation inside the agent pipeline without requiring internal ops teams or ad hoc task management.
